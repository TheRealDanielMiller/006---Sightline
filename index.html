Sightline
Sample data · US 41, Fort Myers

Get to work safely, and be seen on the way.

Sightline plans your walk or ride along busy roads so you avoid the spots that make a commute risky. It picks which side of the street to walk on and where to cross, so turning drivers are already looking your way.

  • 🚧
    Construction zonesClosed sidewalks and work crews change by the hour. We check what's active when you leave.
  • ⛔
    Missing sidewalksGaps that push you onto the shoulder or the grass, worst after dark.
  • 👀
    Right turn on redDrivers turning right on red look left for traffic. We route you so you come from that side, where they'll actually see you.

Why the side of the street matters

East side ✓ The westbound driver turning right on red is looking left, toward you.
West side ! The eastbound driver is looking north for traffic while you come up behind their right shoulder.

Rule of thumb: walk on the right-hand side for your direction of travel, so you move with the traffic beside you.
